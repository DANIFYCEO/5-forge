Good to know

A few common
questions.

Which parts of Abuja do you serve?+

5 FORGE is based in Abuja and serves the FCT. Send your area or estate name when you enquire so we can confirm availability for your location.

Can you help me choose a solar or inverter size?+

Yes. We start with the appliances you need to run, how long you need backup, and the site conditions. The planner above is a rough starting point; final system sizing needs a proper load and site assessment.

Do you work on existing electrical systems?+

Yes. Enquire about repairs, fault finding, wiring changes, distribution boards and preventive maintenance for existing installations.

How do I get a quote?+

Use the project form or WhatsApp button. Share your name, area, the type of work and a short description. The team can advise if a site visit is needed.
